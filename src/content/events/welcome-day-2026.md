On 4 November 2026, INSIGHT officially set sail at ESTIN. The crew presented the club: a student-led
research community, free and open to everyone, built around four research poles and a training path
that takes every member from Rookie to Captain.

The day ended with a live talk by **Dr. Pin-Yu Chen**, Principal Research Scientist at IBM Research,
who joined from New York to share his work on building trustworthy AI: adversarial robustness, the
safety of large language models, and tools to look inside models.
