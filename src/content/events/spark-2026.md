SPARK opens every season at INSIGHT. It introduces members to the fundamentals of scientific research
and gives them the basic skills they need to take part in research activities.

Across five stages, participants go from understanding what research is, to reading papers, to writing
their own, and then bringing the two together. SPARK ends with a research competition where everyone
puts what they learned into practice.
