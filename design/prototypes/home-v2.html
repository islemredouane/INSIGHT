Log 01 · Who we are

A student-led research community at ESTIN.

Run by students, free and open to everyone. No experience required, only curiosity.

“A crew of ESTIN students who read, question and contribute to science.”

Our vision

I

Spark curiosity

Make asking questions a habit.

II

Open the door to research

Introduce students to academic research.

III

Sharpen the mind

Develop research and critical-thinking skills.

IV

Build bridges

Connect students with researchers, labs and the scientific community.
